## About Me

I have been building powerful and dynamic WordPress websites with modern tools
and custom functionalities for the past 4 years. During this journey, I actively
sought deeper knowledge by learning from senior developers and leveraging
extensive online resources.

This experience helped me understand the importance of creating secure, fast,
clean, and real-world web solutions. I enjoy solving client problems by adding
custom PHP/JS functionalities instead of relying on unnecessary heavy plugins.

Currently, I am expanding my horizons by diving deep into **Advanced &
Object-Oriented PHP (OOP)** and modern **JavaScript** to build next-generation
custom WordPress themes and plugins, while leveraging cutting-edge **AI tools**
to optimize workflows and stay ahead of the tech curve.

Beyond that, I am also looking forward to expanding my knowledge of Laravel and
other PHP and JavaScript frameworks.
